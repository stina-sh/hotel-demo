SAMIHA5 — ASTRA HIGH TOP ORNAMENT DRAWINGS / RHINO 7

There are 15 designs, numbered 00–14. Each .3dm contains a flat editable
HALF drawing of the top ornament, in millimeters. The matching PNG is a full mirrored visual preview. The client
can place the top design on their thin ring-sizing circle in MatrixGold.
No ring-sizing circle, under-ring hoop, or plain shank is included.

Layer 01_CLOSED_TOP_PROFILES_REVIEW holds closed native curves.
Layer 02_OPEN_TOP_ORNAMENT holds open native curves.
Layer 03_EDITABLE_GUIDES holds hidden guide curves, where present.

These are source-based 2D drawing candidates, not measured production CAD.
The single angled concept images do not establish exact dimensions or hidden
geometry. Check curve contacts, overlaps, metal thickness, settings, and
manufacturing dimensions in Rhino 7/MatrixGold before building the 3D model.
Original images are available beside each drawing on the published page.
