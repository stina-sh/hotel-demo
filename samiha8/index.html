GEOMETRY, EDITABILITY & SCOPE

Checked dimensions.
Native detail you can refine.

What changed and passed

  • Client-native setting and support geometry replaces the earlier simplified constructions
  • Complete top envelopes: 25.000 mm including 1 mm beads; lower band: 4 mm
  • EU 62 bore datum: approximately 19.7352 mm diameter
  • Nominal 0.2 mm normal-offset hollow head, gallery and shank panels, with per-design finite checks
  • Native validity, bead size/spacing, head clearance and component contact checks
  • Source ZIPs unpacked and replayed: native geometry records matched

For professional finishing

The reference photographs are uncalibrated. These are donor-based design variants, not exact photographic scans. Requested 1 mm grains are coarser than the fine grains in the references.

Components remain separate, contacting or overlapping closed solids for editing. They are not certified as one globally fused manufacturing solid. Junctions and pointed ends may be thicker than nominal panel walls; finite checks are not a global wall proof.

MatrixGold runtime import, shrinkage, full void drainage and manufacturing remain for the jeweller to validate. Pay particular attention to small hollow recesses, especially the smallest design 4 heads. Display colours are not alloy specifications.
