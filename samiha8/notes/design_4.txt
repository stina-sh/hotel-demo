Samiha8 — Design 4

The model retains the donor-based three-ribbon composition and two linked leaf rows. Overall width is 25 mm including the 1 mm border beads. The bottom is 4 mm wide, with an EU62 bore.

The openings under the ribbons are intentional hollow channels. Their shoulder entries follow the sloping ribbon ends. The long outer line is the joined roof and side wall edge of a hollow branch, with positive material-contact evidence.

The Rhino 7 file contains separate, editable, closed solids. It has not been globally Boolean fused. The hollow wall surfaces are nominally 0.2 mm normal offsets; corners and joined regions can be thicker.

The smallest leaf head has an approximately 0.0323 mm central cavity depth. The minimum bead-to-head clearance is approximately 0.010 mm, and nine measured bead-to-bead overlaps remain where rim runs and shared junction stations meet. The maximum overlap depth is 0.110275 mm; the affected sphere centres remain at least 0.889725 mm apart. The rows were visually reviewed as regular and continuous. These are intentional material contacts between separate editable spheres, not a claim that all beads are separated. These details are documented for production review.

Native file validity, dimensions, wall samples and intended contacts have been checked. Manufacturing suitability and Matrix runtime compatibility have not been certified.
