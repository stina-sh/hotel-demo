One workspace.
A connected system.

RedShades is independently developed security engineering software combining a native Qt operator workspace, a Go orchestration runtime, integrated tools and bounded AI decisions. Its product proposition is continuity: retained evidence and explicit work ownership allow useful progress to survive a changing model session, worker or connection.

02 Implemented contract

Five roles, five different responsibilities

The scheduler evaluates declared prerequisites. The Executive observes progress and routes attention or repair. Bounded AI calls interpret a particular evidence packet. The Adaptive Capability Pipeline (ACP) develops a missing interaction candidate. The Autonomic Engineering Plane (AEP) owns a software-repair transaction.

Who decides what happens next?

These roles meet at explicit records. A generated candidate does not become permission, an observation does not become a scheduled action, and a repaired provider does not establish an external outcome. Keeping those distinctions intact is the central integration challenge.

03 Implemented contract

The frontier is the useful unit of progress

A transcript records what was said and attempted. A frontier records what is eligible, what is waiting and which evidence would change that decision. This makes a blocked branch intelligible without requiring every later reader to reconstruct the entire conversation.

Why a long transcript is insufficient

The design also makes failures more precise. A missing prerequisite, an unresolved external effect and a dead worker are different reasons for waiting. Treating them as one generic retry would discard exactly the information needed for recovery.

04 Implemented boundaries; economics under evaluation

Bounded decisions, retained progress

RedShades gives each model decision a defined task and retains the surrounding workspace across decision segments. Accepted results, rejected candidates and supporting artifacts remain available to later work. This creates a basis for matching model capacity to a specific responsibility.

How AI allocation connects to product value

Persistent sessions and phase replay support continuity across attempts. Their economic value should be evaluated through cost per accepted outcome, work retained across interruptions and the number of operator interventions. A universal speed or cost advantage has not yet been established.

05 Implemented contract

One revision, one account of the engagement

Canonical Engagement State links evidence, identities, credentials, routes, artifacts, capabilities, requests and outcomes in a complete revision. Validation checks the relationships before a new checkpoint becomes the head.

What belongs together?

The purpose is consistency across domains. A credential without its realm, a result without its input generation or an artifact without its source cannot safely support the next decision. The canonical foundation is implemented; adoption across legacy execution paths remains incomplete.

06 Implemented contract

History is immutable; the head moves

A generation binds state bytes to identity, parent, revision and timestamp. The persistence boundary verifies the envelope and advances the head conditionally. A concurrent writer cannot silently replace a state it did not observe.

How is a revision published?

This gives recovery a stable reference point. It does not prove that a process is alive or that an external operation finished. Those observations belong to different owners and must be reconciled against the durable record.

07 Implemented contract

Observations need coordinates

An observation becomes useful beyond its original tool when the system retains its source, bytes, context and interpretation separately. Evidence references and digests let later reviewers inspect the material that supported a conclusion.

What makes evidence reusable?

Normalization should preserve disagreement. Two plausible identities or competing explanations must remain distinguishable until a validator or operator has grounds to choose. A cleaner summary is not worth losing the source of uncertainty.

08 Implemented contract

Context is part of the fact

The Execution Context Timeline records factual identity and placement transitions. It separates what was observed from what is currently applicable. A missing context dimension stays unresolved rather than receiving a convenient default.

Why location and identity travel with evidence

This is especially important when one investigation spans several hosts, routes or sessions. The same observation can have different meaning in another context. Context references make that difference visible without embedding sensitive values in every projection.

A graph of eligible work

The inspected source declares 88 automatic stages. The semantic registry also adds 13 common or platform-specific C2 declarations. These are declaration counts, not a promise that every run executes 101 operations.

What does the stage count describe?

Families cover observation, web and identity analysis, evidence review, session-related work and reporting. Prerequisites, platform selection, gates and discovered evidence determine the actual path. The diagram groups those relationships; it is not an execution recipe or a fixed stage order.

10 Implemented contract

A line is not always a dependency

Execution Graph distinguishes hard scheduling dependencies from correlations. Phase barriers, sequential operation order and declared branch dependencies can control eligibility. Evidence links, context links and visual relationships do not gain that authority merely because they appear as edges.

How the graph avoids inventing authority

The scheduler evaluates a bounded stage view. Dynamic endpoint and gate nodes remain useful to the operator without multiplying the work required to decide which stage is ready.

11 Implemented contract

One wait need not stop the investigation

An endpoint review or branch gate has its own scope. Unrelated work can remain eligible while the selected branch waits for evidence or a decision. An explicit global pause is a separate operator action.

How branches progress independently

Dormant work is also distinct from a terminal skip. New evidence may make it relevant later. Preserving that difference avoids both premature completion and endlessly retrying work that has no new input.

12 Implemented contract

Unknown is a state worth keeping

The action coordinator records prepared intent before execution and observes the result before commitment. If execution ownership disappears, the result may be unknown. Automatically retrying would risk repeating an effect that already occurred.

What follows an interrupted effect?

Reconciliation supplies the missing observation or records an explicit recovery decision. This is a more demanding contract than restarting a function, but it is necessary whenever software controls something outside its own memory.

Intelligence with a place to stop

13 Implemented contract

A model call is supervised work

The AI Decision Service receives a revision-bound evidence packet and a registered purpose. Capacity, response size, time and token accounting surround the request. A deterministic validator checks the returned structure and records the accepted result.

What surrounds a decision?

A decision receipt retains the packet revision, model/provider attribution and validation identity. The model does not receive a scheduler mutation API. The boundary is useful because an attractive answer can still be stale, malformed or unsupported.

14 Implemented contract

Supervision needs a view of the whole

The Executive observes the run beyond a single stage. Its role includes diagnosis, prioritization and routing when progress stalls or the current capability set is insufficient. Teacher-mode observation belongs to this wider supervisory design.

What is the Executive watching?

Observation must remain distinguishable from permission and completion. A supervisor can identify where help is needed; accepted state changes still require the relevant validation and policy boundaries.

15 Implemented boundaries; model economics unproven

Keep the task small; keep the history durable

Short decisions become more useful when their inputs are complete and their accepted outputs survive. The surrounding software can retain source artifacts, rejected candidates and progress records instead of asking a model to remember everything in its next context window.

How could a compact model contribute?

This is a testable engineering proposition, not a measured general advantage. Novel synthesis may still require more model capability. Comparing configurations requires the same task, model policy and acceptance boundary; a stronger embedded model changes the experiment.

16 Implemented contract

One capability, one convergence history

ACP retains a capability workspace and thread across decomposition, catalog, binding, SDK, adapter and replica segments. Each segment records input and artifact digests. The session can accumulate correction without distributing the convergence problem across disconnected conversations.

Why persist a generation session?

An exact compatible catalog result or SDK can skip corresponding generation work. Reuse depends on validated receipts and input identity. Live-effect validation remains necessary even when the artifact worked elsewhere.

17 Implemented contract

A candidate appears as a complete artifact set

Adapter source, metadata and optional tests become visible through one manifest that binds their digests. The controller can reject an incomplete generation as an artifact defect rather than repeatedly asking a model where a missing file went.

What crosses the generation boundary?

Rejection receipts remain contract-scoped regression inputs. Keeping the entire rejection history is how the next correction learns from earlier failures after a controller swap or session continuation.

18 Implemented contract

Review should not inherit the generator’s assumptions

The contamination reviewer receives a read-only candidate, contract and provenance categories in a separate workspace. It does not inherit the generation transcript or a walkthrough. Its verdict informs admission but cannot edit the candidate or authorize execution.

Why isolate the contamination reviewer?

Counterfactual review asks whether changed names, ordering, missing evidence and competing candidates would preserve correct behavior. This tests generality more directly than searching only for obvious target strings. It still needs precise grounding and does not guarantee all contamination is detected.

19 Implemented contract

A missing interaction is not a broken platform

A capability gap concerns an interaction the run cannot currently express. A software defect concerns the machinery that should already handle a supported contract. Sending both to the same generator confuses product repair with capability synthesis.

Where ACP ends and AEP begins

AEP covers incident diagnosis, repair ownership, scoped source work, validation, admission, replacement and verified hand-back. AEP success requires that complete loop; a source patch or passing unit test alone is insufficient.

20 Proposed architecture

Specialists are a deployment hypothesis

A shared compact base with resident QLoRA specialists could align model selection with existing phase boundaries. An authoritative controller could choose the specialist for a session segment while review remains isolated.

What is proposed for model allocation?

This catalog has not verified a trained bank, a hundred adapters, cross-adapter KV compatibility or benchmarked transitions. Those require model artifacts, serving support and controlled evaluation. The source boundaries motivate the direction; they do not establish it.

Replaceable work, explicit ownership

21 Implemented contract

Replace behavior without moving truth

The Runtime Composition Kernel governs provider publication, generations, leases, rollback and cleanup. Engagement state remains outside the provider. A replacement therefore changes an implementation boundary without becoming a migration of domain truth.

What does the composition kernel own?

This reduces the number of special lifecycle paths that each worker must invent. It also exposes a hard limit: atomic publication says which provider is visible, not whether an external action completed.

Reference 21 · Implemented contract
22 Implemented contract

Existing work keeps its implementation

A lease identifies the exact provider generation used by a consumer. New work can see a replacement while existing work drains on the prior generation. No transaction silently changes implementation halfway through its lifetime.

Why pin a provider generation?

A swap window can commit or roll back publication. Cleanup remains owned and observable. A rollback cannot undo an external outcome merely by republishing the old provider; effects need their own reconciliation.

23 Implemented contract

Workers return results under a lease

Isolated stage workers receive immutable execution envelopes. Results are accepted only against the current artifact, input and generation lease. That prevents an old worker from publishing a plausible result after its authority has expired.

How is stale work rejected?

In-process handlers and isolated workers have different replacement boundaries. The catalog describes the common principle without implying that every component can be replaced at any arbitrary instruction.

24 Implemented; integrated validation ongoing

A PID is not enough

Process supervision distinguishes a numeric identifier from the exact process generation owned by the application. Cancellation, replacement and cleanup must retain the authority needed to settle owned work without touching unrelated processes.

What does cleanup need to know?

Recent generic lifecycle work includes bounded output capture, joined shutdown and retained cleanup handles. Focused contract evidence supports specific cases; broader integration and clean release acceptance are still in progress.

25 Implemented contract

Existence is not health

A service record can identify a process without proving that it serves its declared purpose. Readiness requires a named, bounded observation. Probe ownership is separate from the retained service process it observes.

When is a resource actually available?

That distinction matters during interruption: stopping a probe should not accidentally terminate the service, and a stale readiness result should not authorize new work indefinitely.

Tools are inputs to a system

External tools, custom workers, brokers and source viewers have different capabilities and output shapes. Integration value comes from attribution, bounded execution and normalization into canonical evidence, not the number of executable names in a menu.

What makes a tool catalog more than a list?

Network, web, identity, directory, browser and source-inspection families feed that record. Installed descriptors can also report manual-required capabilities. Availability is distinct from permission to use an effect.

Artifacts have a lifecycle too

Artifact builders and transformation systems produce versioned material with provenance. A digest identifies bytes; role and generation records explain why those bytes belong to a particular operation or deployment.

What binds a binary to its use?

The same distinction applies to implants and helpers. A built artifact is not a registered session. A verified session is not permission for every later action. The interfaces preserve those separate claims.

How is post-foothold work represented?

The parent run also retains the attachment status. Reporting cannot treat the absence of a local worker counter as proof that attached work completed. The public diagram shows ownership and completion dependencies, without prescribing an operational chain.

A child investigation needs a parent

Routes and child subchains create new scopes of work whose relationship to the parent must remain explicit. Context, lineage and completion dependencies prevent a new branch from becoming an untracked side effect.

The useful abstraction is a graph of attributed investigations. The route or session that enables a child is evidence with its own validity, not a permanent assumption attached to a host name.

39 Implemented contract

A passing component is a beginning

Focused tests establish narrow contracts. Integrated simulations exercise connected boundaries. Race, repetition, fuzz and fault lanes look for behavior that an ordinary happy path can miss. A clean whole-system run asks a different question again.

How should confidence accumulate?

None of these should be substituted for another. A replay that inherits repaired state is useful diagnostic evidence, but it cannot establish the same claim as a fresh run without outer repair.

40 Implemented checks; release acceptance pending

The receipt is part of the test

Ultra admission binds required lanes to the revision, command and retained outputs. Missing, skipped, unavailable or malformed results cannot be treated as passing. A configured test inventory is separate from an executed receipt.

What does green mean?

The documented release rule requires two clean hermetic runs, with only declared immutable caches reused. Current expanded validation remains in progress; this publication does not certify a green release.

41 Retained study

Coverage of a vocabulary is not discovery

The Trigger Methodology Graph study grouped 200 public disclosures into 37 recurring families. Of 40 identity-disjoint held-out disclosures, 39 reused a training family. That is family coverage, not 39 findings produced by RedShades.

What did the disclosure study measure?

The cohort was curated and did not enforce a strict chronological split. Synthetic calibration recorded 600 activations; zero candidates were live-exercised or admitted in the reported study. Those limits define what the result can support.

42 Implemented contract

A hypothesis should reveal its missing evidence

Trigger Methodology Graph (TMG) produces evidence-bound diagnostic hypotheses. Shadow Capability Graph (SCG) reasons about missing prerequisites from an immutable snapshot. Bounty Hunting Gates (BHG) retain policy review for proposed hypotheses.

How do TMG and SCG contribute?

Their useful role is to make uncertainty and the next required evidence explicit. A diagnostic graph does not become scheduling or execution authority simply because its suggestion is plausible.

43 Developer-reported autonomous outcomes

Historical outcomes deserve precise attribution

The developer reports autonomous completion of P.O.O, one hard Windows CTF, one insane Windows HTB lab and roughly two easy Linux/web HTB labs. These are reported unattended outcomes, not assisted runs.

What has been reported?

A complete independent log bundle for each outcome was not established in this editorial review. Subsequent capability expansion is still being stabilized. The historical results therefore should not be read as proof of generalization to unseen environments.

44 Active stabilization

The difficult part is still integration

Canonical-state adoption, composed lifecycle recovery, artifact startup checks and complete release acceptance still require evidence beyond individual contract tests. Interrupted effects and changing environments remain especially demanding boundaries.

Where confidence remains incomplete

There is also no established benchmark here for compact-model speed, specialist-bank quality or cost per successful unseen task. Those claims need controlled measurements with the same acceptance criteria and explicit intervention accounting.

Reference 44 · Active stabilization
45 Development direction

Make the next result easier to audit

The next valuable evidence is repeated fresh acceptance with exact model routing, complete failure history and independently checked outcomes. That would show whether the architecture converts local repairs into reliable accumulated progress.

What would strengthen the proposition?

Further training work should follow those boundaries: start from actual task and rejection traces, evaluate specialist transitions, and measure whether the same result needs fewer interventions. Adding more experts before that evidence would increase complexity without proving value.
