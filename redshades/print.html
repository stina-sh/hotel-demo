The product and engineering proposition

RedShades. Intelligence with an operating system.

An 88-stage adaptive chain, custom capability generation, mid-run software repair and a native Qt workspace connect in one independently developed platform. RedShades combines deterministic orchestration with focused AI reasoning to carry complex security investigations across tools, branches and long-running sessions.

The central proposition is architectural: spend model capacity on interpretation and synthesis while software owns workflow, context, validation and execution policy. The 88 automatic declarations form a conditional graph; separate C2 stage families extend session work.

Who decides what happens next?

A chain that schedules, branches and adapts

The scheduler evaluates 88 automatic stage declarations against current evidence. Independent branches advance when their inputs are ready; loops revisit useful frontiers as observations change. The Executive maintains the wider view while ACP supplies missing capabilities and AEP repairs the underlying software.

Each role has a distinct responsibility: scheduling, supervision, bounded interpretation, capability generation or platform repair. Their shared records let the investigation progress beyond one prompt or one tool.

How AI allocation connects to product value

70% deterministic. 30% neural.

RedShades uses this ratio as a design philosophy: dependable software surrounds the places where model judgment adds value. Scheduling, state, policy and validation provide structure; models interpret ambiguity, develop candidates and guide complex engineering work.

The ratio is a design framing, not a measured code, runtime or success percentage. The intended economic advantage is selective intelligence: compact specialists for bounded decisions, persistent sessions for work that benefits from continuity.

How is stale work rejected?

Workers return results under a lease

Isolated stage workers receive immutable execution envelopes. Results are accepted only against the current artifact, input and generation lease. That prevents an old worker from publishing a plausible result after its authority has expired.

In-process handlers and isolated workers have different replacement boundaries. The catalog describes the common principle without implying that every component can be replaced at any arbitrary instruction.

Disclosures become a reusable methodology vocabulary

How do TMG and SCG contribute?

A hypothesis should reveal its missing evidence

Trigger Methodology Graph (TMG) produces evidence-bound diagnostic hypotheses. Shadow Capability Graph (SCG) reasons about missing prerequisites from an immutable snapshot. Bounty Hunting Gates (BHG) retain policy review for proposed hypotheses.

Their useful role is to make uncertainty and the next required evidence explicit. A diagnostic graph does not become scheduling or execution authority simply because its suggestion is plausible.
