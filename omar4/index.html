Critical engineering boundary

Isolation is not
interruption.

A disconnector normally creates a visible isolation gap after a circuit breaker or other rated interrupting device has stopped load or fault current. It must never be assumed capable of breaking load current unless a specific tested load-break design says so.

Interlocks
